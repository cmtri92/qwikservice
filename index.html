Nexora annual vehicle safety inspection

Most vehicles in Nexora must pass a safety inspection every 12 months. Quinn books you only at certified inspection centers.

How it works

  • Visit an official inspection station
  • Old sticker is removed
  • Certified inspector checks safety items
  • Pass: a new windshield sticker is issued

What is inspected

  • Brakes & parking brake
  • Lights, signals & horn
  • Steering, suspension, tires & wheels
  • Mirrors, glass, wipers, seat belts
  • Exhaust, fuel & emissions equipment

If you don't pass

  • A rejection sticker or notice may be issued
  • Repair the listed defects
  • Return for reinspection within 15 days

Frequently asked questions

You can also simply ask Quinn. No verification needed for general questions.

Can I change my service center location?

Yes. You can usually change locations before final confirmation, though you may need to reselect available services or times.

Will I get a confirmation email or SMS?

Yes. A confirmation is typically sent by email, and optional text reminders may be available.

What time should I arrive for my service?

Arrive about 10–15 minutes early so check-in is quick and easy.

Are loaner vehicles available during service?

Loaner availability varies by location and service type. Check with your service center when booking.

Can I book if I am not sure what my vehicle needs?

Yes. Choose a general inspection or diagnostic visit and discuss the symptoms with the technician.
